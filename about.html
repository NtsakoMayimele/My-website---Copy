About Us

Rise Up Hoops Foundation is based in Polokwane. We offer after-school community basketball programmes for young people from disadvantaged communities. We focus on skills training, mentorship and academic support.

Our Mission

To promote basketball as a way of keeping young people in school and away from negative influences.

Our Vision

To have a coached and organised basketball programme in every township in Limpopo by 2030.
